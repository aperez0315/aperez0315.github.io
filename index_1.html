Why This Matters

The gap isn't data. It's the skillset to use it.

Most small businesses can't justify a full-time data hire, so decisions get made on gut feel or scattered spreadsheets. The gap isn't a lack of data — it's not having someone with statistical training on hand to structure and interpret it well. That's the gap this fills: organizing what you already have, applying real statistical methods, and — where it makes sense — building lightweight AI tools so the insight keeps showing up without paying for a full analyst every time.

What I Bring

Pick what your business needs.

These aren't sequential steps — they're independent services. Depending on how organized (or not) your data already is, you may only need one or two, not all four.

Organize

Structure the mess

Take scattered, messy, or siloed data — spreadsheets, POS exports, HR systems, whatever exists — and structure it into something reliable enough to actually analyze.

Analyze

Find the real signal

Apply real statistical methods to find what's actually driving results — churn, cost, demand, performance — and separate signal from noise.

Visualize

Build the dashboard

Turn the analysis into an interactive dashboard in Tableau, R Shiny, or Streamlit — so the answer stays live instead of sitting in a static report.

Automate

Keep the insight coming

Build AI agents that keep answering your business questions on an ongoing basis, in plain language, without needing a full-time analyst.
